The Problem & Solution

Remove the upload step.
The platform fills itself.

Every semester, students and guides lose hundreds of hours scrolling group chats hunting for documents.

University portals assume that students will willingly log in, convert formats, tag subjects, and upload documents. In reality, adoption hovers near zero because communication has already taken place in chat.

Reyna inverts the funnel: the Android client watches the folder WhatsApp writes to. When write finishes, Reyna verifies novelty, files it into the student's own Google Drive, and correlates signals to remember who shared it.

Architecture Sequence

One system.
Built in four resilient layers.

You cannot reason over what you cannot remember. Reyna builds on an unbreakable chain of trust.

1

Capture is the Floor

Android FileObserver watches the local WhatsApp media tree. Files are ingested on CLOSE_WRITE. Zero battery drain, zero polling.

2

Attribution is the Proof

Reconstructs the sender from four independent signals: timestamps, naming patterns, sent directory, and chat exports. Strictly refuses to guess below 0.70.

3

Storage is Sovereign

Files stream straight to the student's personal Google Drive. The Go server stores only metadata and text caches. Zero documents on central servers.

4

Retrieval is Conversational

Office documents are parsed locally for zero cost. Whole-word tokenized BM25 ranking + Gemini 2.5 Flash produces answers with clickable verified Sources.

The Forensic Principle

Never guess a name.
A wrong name is worse than no name.

A file on disk carries no author header. Reyna grades attribution confidence into three clear visual tiers.

Score ≥ 0.70
🟢 "Priya · 18 Aug"

High confidence. Proven by matching chat export timestamps or directional sent traces. Reyna confidently names the sender.

Score 0.30 – 0.69
🟠 "Sem 5 CS · 18 Aug"

Evidence isolates the group chat, but cannot prove the individual member. Reyna names the chat and refuses to guess a person.

Score < 0.30
⚪ "Found on your phone"

Ambiguous signals. Reyna explicitly states the document was discovered locally on the device without inventing an attribution.
